    On iPhone or iPad: tap Share ⬆︎, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen, like an app.

    Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left to move, push to the edge to sprint
    SHOOT: hold to charge, let go to shoot — steer while charging to curl
    With the ball: SHOOT · PASS · THROUGH (into space) · SKILL (sidestep; pull back to turn)
    Ball in the air: SHOOT to head or volley it · PASS to head it to a teammate
    Defending: TACKLE · SWITCH (to the Q badge) · PRESS (hold to chase) · SPRINT
    WASD / Arrows move
    Shift sprint
    J / Space hold to shoot — steer sideways to curl
    K pass · I through ball (into space)
    L tackle · hold E to press (chase the ball)
    O skill move: sidestep, or pull back to drag it back · beats a tackle
    Ball in the air: J heads or volleys it · K heads it to a teammate · crosses come in from out wide

    Esc pause · M sound
